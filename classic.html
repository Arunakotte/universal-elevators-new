07Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about elevator installation, maintenance contracts, pitless requirements, and safety protocols in Bangalore.

Yes, absolutely! We specialize in home elevator retrofits. We can install compact pitless elevators inside the stairwell cut-out or construct an external glass facade shaft against an exterior wall. Our solutions require minimal civil work, no deep pit (pit depths as low as 150mm to 300mm), and can run on single-phase domestic power supply.
Our AMC plans include monthly comprehensive maintenance visits (lubrication, brake adjustment, door sensor checks, safety switch testing), 24/7 breakdown assistance, periodic rope and traction checks, and ARD battery load testing. We offer both Non-Comprehensive (service labor included) and Comprehensive (service labor + major spare parts covered) plans.
Every Universal Elevators installation is equipped with an Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) and emergency battery backup. In the event of a Bangalore grid power failure, the ARD instantly activates, smoothly guides the cabin to the nearest floor, opens the doors automatically, and enables passengers to walk out safely.
Typically, manufacturing and fabrication take 3 to 4 weeks depending on customized finishes (panoramic glass, custom cabin stainless steel). Site erection, rail alignment, and commissioning take approximately 10 to 14 days after the site shaft is handed over ready. We provide a detailed schedule upfront.
All our equipment conforms strictly to Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) IS 14665, the Karnataka Lifts, Escalators and Passenger Conveyors Act, and international EN 81 safety guidelines. Safety elements include mechanical overspeed governors, safety gear brakes, full-height infrared door sensors, interlocked landing doors, and emergency alarms.
