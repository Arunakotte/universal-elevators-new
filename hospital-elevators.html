Hospital & Stretcher Bed Elevators

Uncompromised precision, sterile hygiene, and jerkless transit engineered for healthcare centres, nursing homes, and diagnostic facilities in Bangalore.

01Product Overview

Smooth, Sterile & Critical Medical Transit

In emergency medical situations, seconds count and ride comfort is non-negotiable. Universal Elevators manufactures specialized hospital stretcher elevators tailored to Indian and international hospital requirements.

Our cabins feature extended depths (2400 mm) to effortlessly accommodate standard wheeled patient stretchers, life-support trolleys, doctors, and nursing staff. Advanced closed-loop VVVF drives ensure exact zero-jerk starts, travel, and millimetre-precise floor levelling to protect post-operative and trauma patients.

Healthcare-Specific Features

Medical Emergency Override: Key-operated emergency recall priority switch.
Antibacterial SS 304 Surfaces: Seamless medical-grade stainless steel walls.
Dual Full-Length Handrails & Bumpers: Protective PVC wall bumpers against stretcher impact.
Smooth Levelling: Floor alignment within ±2mm to eliminate stretcher vibration.
