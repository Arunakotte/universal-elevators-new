03Standard of Excellence

Our 28-Point Monthly Inspection Protocol

Every monthly service visit covers thorough electrical, mechanical, and safety examinations.

1. Drive & Machine Room

  • • Gearbox & bearing lubrication levels
  • • Traction motor insulation & thermal audit
  • • Electromagnetic brake pad gap & tension
  • • Main drive sheave groove wear check
  • • Microprocessor controller error log review

2. Hoistway & Suspension

  • • Steel wire rope tension equalization
  • • Rope crown wear & lubrication inspection
  • • Counterweight shoe clearance
  • • Guide rail lubrication & bracket tightness
  • • Travelling cable slack & sheath condition

3. Car & Door Operators

  • • Full-height infrared door curtain sensitivity
  • • Door clutch & interlock contact engagement
  • • Door drive belt tension & skate alignment
  • • Car top inspection station switches
  • • Emergency alarm & intercom voice clarity

4. Safety Devices & Pit

  • • Automatic Rescue Device (ARD) discharge test
  • • Overspeed governor mechanical trip check
  • • Car safety wedge brake drop mechanism
  • • Pit emergency stop switch & buffer stroke
  • • Final terminal limit switch tripping
