Luxury Villa Panoramic Elevators

Architectural statement lifts combining 360-degree curved glass elegance, custom titanium accents, and world-class whisper quiet engineering.

01Product Overview

The Crown Jewel of Bangalore Luxury Living

Universal Elevators’ Villa Series represents the pinnacle of luxury vertical transportation. Designed for premium bungalows, penthouses, and gated villa communities in Whitefield, Sadashivanagar, and Koramangala, these lifts elevate your home’s architectural value into a living work of art.

Crafted with ultra-clear tempered laminated security glass, concealed drive tracks, and custom finishes in rose gold, titanium black, or champagne bronze, each villa elevator is manufactured to order to seamlessly integrate with your interior design language.

Key Highlights

360° Panoramic Vision: Curved or frameless glass walls offering unobstructed views.
Designer Car Operating Panels: Touch-sensitive crystal glass COP with OLED display.
Zero Structural Intrusion: Self-supporting glass and powder-coated steel towers.
Whisper Smooth Ride: Sub-40dB acoustic profile with active levelling.
