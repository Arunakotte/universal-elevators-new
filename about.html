01Our Journey

A Decade of Vertical Engineering Precision

Founded with the resolute belief that an elevator is not just an appliance but the vital backbone of every building, Universal Elevators has emerged as one of Bangalore’s premier elevator manufacturers, installers, and service providers.

Over our 10+ years of operational history, we have successfully completed over 500 installations spanning private residential villas, upscale apartment complexes, healthcare institutions, commercial business parks, and heavy-duty industrial warehouses throughout Bangalore and Karnataka.

Our tagline, "Trust for a Joyful Journey," isn't just a corporate slogan — it represents the peace of mind experienced by families and building occupants knowing their elevator was engineered with triple-redundant safety mechanisms, whisper-quiet mechanics, and rigorous preventive care.

03Quality Standards

Regulatory & Engineering Compliance

Universal Elevators designs and inspects every installation under rigorous domestic and global elevator safety codes.

BIS IS 14665 Compliance Complete adherence to Bureau of Indian Standards specifications for electric traction & hydraulic lifts.
Karnataka Lift Act Conformity Facilitation of statutory inspections and official licenses through the Karnataka Electrical Inspectorate.
European EN 81-20/50 Guidelines Modern design guidelines for enhanced passenger clearance, refuge spaces, and electrical monitoring systems.
Green Energy PMSM Traction Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors that reduce energy consumption by up to 40% compared to traditional geared drives.
